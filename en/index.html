🔥 Grill Me — extract what's in your head

Everyone uses the same model. What makes your output sound like you it's not the prompt — it's the context that you inject. But the context is stuck in your head.

O grill-me is a skill that no-holds-barred interview — one question at a time, saving each answer to disk—until everything in your head becomes a reusable document. This course teaches the fundamentals, the techniques, how the skill works, and how to apply it in practice.

The grill-me cycle: knowledge leaves your head through questions, and each answer is recorded in the capture file (the source of truth), and the loop repeats until no gaps remain.

What it is, in one sentence

🎯

A relentless interviewer

Asks one question at a time, always with its recommended answer, and walks through every branch of the decision tree until you reach a shared understanding.

📄

A file that never forgets

With each answer, it asks checkpoint on disk — in brainstorms/. The context window fills up; the file is the source of truth.

⚙️

Fuel for your system

In the end, what was extracted updates your skills e docs. You come out of iteration 1 at around 90% instead of 70%.
